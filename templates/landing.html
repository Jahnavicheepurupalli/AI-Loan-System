Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Common banking and verification system queries answered instantly.

How does the Live Face Verification work?
The biometric module uses your camera to scan face parameters in real-time, matching it against the image detected on your uploaded Aadhaar card using a 95%+ confidence metric.
Can I reapply if my application is rejected?
Yes. If rejected, our Groq AI Engine generates structured mitigation points (e.g. reducing requested capital or clearing EMIs) to help you qualify for approval.
Are my files kept secure?
All documents are processed under strict JWT token boundaries. Upload signatures are encrypted and checked for duplication or metadata anomalies in compliance with security guidelines.
